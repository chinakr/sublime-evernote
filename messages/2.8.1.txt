Evernote for Sublime Text v2.8.1
================================

Thanks for updating the Evernote plugin!

Highlights of v2.8.1:

 * Package Control submission hygiene: the menu now offers proper
   "Settings" and "Key Bindings" entries (edit_settings) so they are
   easier to find and customize.
 * Fixed static review warnings about hidden console windows when
   spawning helper processes (no behavior change on macOS/Linux;
   Windows already used os.startfile).

If you encounter any problem, open an issue at
https://github.com/chinakr/sublime-evernote/issues
and enable the "debug" setting to collect console output.
